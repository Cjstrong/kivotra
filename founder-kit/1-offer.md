# The Offer — the only thing we sell

**The Kivotra Launchpad. €4,500 fixed. 30 days. Paid upfront.**
Founding rate: **€3,500 for the first three clients**, in exchange for a written testimonial and case-study rights. After three, the rate is gone forever.

Everything else in the blueprint (retainers, audits, scores, tiers) is **parked** until three Launchpads are delivered and paid. One offer until it's boring.

## Who it's for

An Irish founder of a B2B or professional-services firm whose Google page 1 doesn't match their competence. They're about to raise, enter a market, or chase bigger clients — and buyers check them first.

## What they get (30 days, three pieces, honestly labelled)

1. **One national editorial feature** — *paid placement: publication guaranteed via the outlet's sponsored programme, disclosed per outlet policy.*
   A full feature about the founder on **USA Today, Entrepreneur.com, or Complex.com** (we recommend the best fit). Interviewed, written by us, two revision rounds, submitted, published.
2. **One press release, distributed** — *distribution guaranteed; journalist coverage is not.*
   Their strongest current announcement, drafted and distributed across a national wire network, with a syndication URL report.
3. **Authority assets** — *owned, theirs forever.*
   Positioning one-pager · three bios (25/100/250 words) · press-page copy for their site · LinkedIn profile rewrite · amplification kit (3 posts, banner, "Featured in" badge usage rules).

## The two honesty lines (say them in every pitch)

- **We never promise:** rankings, traffic, leads, sales, or earned coverage. That's exactly why you can believe the rest.
- **The guarantee:** if we cannot get your feature published on any of the three outlets, you get a full refund.

## Why paid upfront

We order the placement (hard cost ≈ €1,400) the day payment lands. No upfront, no slot.

## Unit economics (keep this honest with ourselves)

€3,500 founding / €4,500 list − ~€1,550 hard costs (placement + wire) = €1,950–€2,950 per client before our time (~25 hrs). Three founding clients ≈ €10,500 in, ≈ €4,650 out. That's the runway.
