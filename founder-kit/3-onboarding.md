# Onboarding — payment to production in 5 days

## Day 0 — payment lands

Move CRM row to `Won`. Send the welcome email the same hour:

> **Subject: Kivotra — you're in. Two things to start.**
>
> {FirstName} — payment received, your 30 days start today. We've ordered your placement slot.
>
> Two things from you this week:
>
> **1. Book your story session** — 75 minutes, recorded, just us talking: {booking link or three proposed times}. Everything we write comes from this one conversation. No homework, no forms after this.
>
> **2. Reply to this email with:**
> 1. Company: founded when, headcount, what you actually sell and to whom
> 2. Your three proudest client outcomes — and for each, whether we can name the client (yes/no)
> 3. Any press, awards, or talks you already have (links)
> 4. The announcement you'd make tomorrow if you had a megaphone (this becomes your press release)
> 5. A headshot you don't hate, highest resolution you have
> 6. Anything we must NOT say (competitors, numbers, clients, pending deals)
> 7. Who approves copy (one name — you, ideally) and confirm you can turn approvals around in 48 hours. That 48-hour promise is what makes the 30-day promise possible.
>
> — {CJ / Cristian}

## Day 1–2 — the story session (75 min, recorded)

Cover, in order: origin story → the contrarian opinion they hold about their industry → three client stories with numbers → what they want to be known for in 3 years → the announcement. Get the transcript (Meet/Zoom auto-transcribe — free).

## Day 3 — angles

Send three one-paragraph angle memos for the feature. Each memo: the headline we'd pitch, the story it tells, why a stranger would keep reading. Ask them to pick one by Day 5 (their 48-hour SLA starts here).

## Day 5 — locked

Angle chosen, release facts confirmed. Delivery system takes over (`4-delivery.md`). If they miss the 48-hour approval twice, we call them — approval lag is the only thing that can break the 30-day promise, and we say exactly that, warmly.
