# The Sales Process — one ritual, 4 hours a week

**The math:** 10 snapshots/week → ~2 calls → ~1 close per fortnight. If 40 snapshots produce 0 closes, we change the offer, not the effort.

## Monday (60 min): list 10 named founders

Sources, in order of quality: award shortlists (they're already investing in visibility), Enterprise Ireland / LEO client news, funding announcements, LinkedIn. Add all 10 to the CRM as `To contact`.

## Tuesday–Thursday (20 min each): the manual snapshot

No tooling yet — by hand, that's the point at €0:

1. Google their name + company in an incognito window. Screenshot page 1.
2. Ask ChatGPT and Perplexity: *"Who is {Name}, founder of {Company}? Would you recommend them for {what they sell}?"* Screenshot the answers.
3. Write three honest observations (what's missing, what's wrong, what's invisible).

Send this email. **The snapshot is the pitch — there is no pitch.**

> **Subject: what ChatGPT says about you, {FirstName}**
>
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> Before anyone buys from {Company}, they check you out — on Google, and increasingly by asking ChatGPT.
>
> I ran both checks on you this morning. Attached: what page 1 of Google shows, and what ChatGPT actually says when asked if you can be trusted with {what they sell}.
>
> Three things stood out:
> 1. {observation}
> 2. {observation}
> 3. {observation}
>
> I run Kivotra, an authority agency in Dublin — we fix exactly this. If the screenshots bother you as much as they'd bother me, I'll walk you through what I'd do about it. 30 minutes, no deck.
>
> {CJ / Cristian}

**Follow-up, once, after 4 days:**

> Subject: re: what ChatGPT says about you
>
> {FirstName} — one thought I forgot: {one new specific observation}. Still happy to walk you through it. If it's not a priority right now, say so and I'll leave you be.

Then move the row to `Nurture` or `Lost`. Never a third email.

## The call (30 min, one call, close on it)

- **0–5:** their story — what they sell, who's buying, what's coming (raise? market entry?).
- **5–15:** walk the snapshot. Let the screenshots do the selling. Ask: "when a buyer sees this, what do they conclude?"
- **15–20:** what good looks like — show our own press page and one example feature. Label it honestly: *"this is a sponsored editorial programme — publication is guaranteed, and it's disclosed. What's not guaranteed is rankings or leads, and anyone who promises you those is lying."*
- **20–28:** the offer. €4,500 fixed — €3,500 founding rate for the first three, testimonial and case study in exchange. 30 days. Paid upfront because we order your placement the day payment lands. Full refund if the feature can't be published.
- **28–30:** close: *"I'll send a one-pager and the payment link today. Your 30 days start when payment lands."*

## The only three objections

- **"Can you guarantee coverage?"** — "I can guarantee the feature and the distribution, because those are paid programmes and I'll show you exactly which. I can't guarantee journalists write about you — nobody can, and the ones who say otherwise are selling you syndication dressed up as news."
- **"€4,500 is a lot."** — "It's less than one lost deal. And it's a fifth of what a PR retainer costs in a year — with nothing guaranteed at the end of it."
- **"Let me think about it."** — "Do. The founding rate exists for three clients; after that it's €4,500. The work is the same either way."

## Same day as the call

Send the proposal one-pager (see `5-proposal.md`) + payment link (Stripe payment link or bank transfer — both free to set up). Move CRM row to `Proposal sent`, set `Next date` +3 days.
